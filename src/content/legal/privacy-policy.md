For personal data collected through this website, we are the *Data Fiduciary*.

## 2. What we collect

**Through the enquiry form:** your name, phone number, email address (optional), the service you are interested in and your message.

**When you call or WhatsApp us:** your phone number, name and whatever you choose to share in the conversation.

**When you visit the website:** basic, anonymous usage statistics (pages visited, device type, approximate region). We use privacy-friendly analytics that do not use cookies and do not identify you.

Please do not send PAN, Aadhaar, bank details or financial documents through the website form. We will arrange a secure way to receive documents once you engage us.

## 3. Why we use it

- To reply to your enquiry and discuss the service you asked about.
- To send you due-date reminders, **only** if you tick the separate box agreeing to this.
- To keep a record of your consent, as the law requires.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

## 4. Legal basis

We process enquiry data on the basis of your **consent**, given by ticking the box on the form. We may also process data for legitimate uses permitted under Section 7 of the Act, such as complying with a legal obligation.

## 5. Who we share it with

Only the service providers that help us run this website and respond to you:

| Service | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Website hosting provider | Serves the website |
| Form delivery service | Sends form submissions to our email |
| Google (Gmail) | Our email inbox |
| Cookieless analytics provider | Anonymous visit statistics |
| Google Maps | Shows our location, only if you tap "Show map" |

These providers process data on our instructions. We may also disclose data where the law requires it.

## 6. How long we keep it

- Enquiries that do not become client engagements: deleted **12 months** after the last contact.
- If you become a client, your records are kept for as long as tax and company laws require.
- Consent records: kept for as long as needed to show that consent was given.

## 7. How we protect it

The website uses HTTPS. Form submissions go straight to our email and are not stored on the web server. Our email account is protected by two-factor sign-in, and access is limited to people who need it. If a personal data breach occurs, we will inform the Data Protection Board of India and affected people as the law requires.

## 8. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act, you can:

- **Access** a summary of the personal data we hold about you.
- **Correct or update** inaccurate or incomplete data.
- **Erase** data that is no longer needed, subject to legal retention requirements.
- **Withdraw consent** at any time. Email us or send "STOP" on WhatsApp. Withdrawal does not affect anything done before it.
- **Nominate** another person to exercise your rights if you are unable to.
- **Raise a grievance** with our Grievance Officer.

To use any of these rights, contact the Grievance Officer below.
